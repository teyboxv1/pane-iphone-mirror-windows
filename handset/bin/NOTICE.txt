Pane bundles an optional AirPlay audio receiver binary for Windows.

When handset/bin/shairport.exe is present, it is derived from Shairport4w
(https://github.com/Frank-Friemel/Shairport4w), Copyright Frank Friemel and
contributors, licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 or later.

Place the Shairport4w release binary at handset/bin/shairport.exe if you
wish to enable the Pane Audio AirPlay output. The main Pane application
remains GPL-3.0; see LICENSE in the repository root.
